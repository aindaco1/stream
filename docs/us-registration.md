# U.S. marketplace registration before live sales

Reviewed November 20, 2026 for Dust Wave’s U.S. film rentals and downloads. This guide assumes the platform operates in New Mexico, takes viewer payments and pays filmmakers. Use the operating legal entity’s actual sales history, business locations and existing permits when deciding where to register.

## Before the first paid screening

1. **Check Dust Wave’s New Mexico account.** In Taxpayer Access Point, confirm the operating entity’s New Mexico Business Tax Identification Number, active gross receipts tax account, marketplace-provider information, business location and filing schedule. If the entity already uses that account for Store or Pool, check whether the activity or location needs updating. Registration is free. Physical presence in New Mexico means the remote-seller threshold does not give an Albuquerque business a grace period. Keep the documentation a filmmaker needs for the marketplace deduction. [New Mexico registration](https://www.tax.newmexico.gov/businesses/who-must-register-a-business/) · [Marketplace sales guidance](https://www.tax.newmexico.gov/businesses/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2021/09/FYI-206.pdf)
2. **Check the operating address.** A business inside Albuquerque city limits generally needs a current city business license, including a home-based business. Verify the license covers this location and activity. The city asks for the state registration certificate with the physical business address. Use the actual address to determine the licensing jurisdiction. [Albuquerque licensing FAQ and application](https://www.cabq.gov/planning/planning-faqs/business-registration-faqs)
3. **Screen the other states and D.C. before opening checkout nationally.** Inventory existing permits, offices, employees, inventory, other in-state activities, and where the filmmakers operate. Combine direct and facilitated sales of the same legal entity as required by each jurisdiction, including relevant Store, Pool and off-Stripe sales. Review revenue, transaction counts, lookback periods, start dates and whether streaming rentals and permanent downloads are taxable. Independent filmmakers can affect the answer in some states. For example, Washington lists a marketplace seller’s physical presence in Washington as a registration trigger for a facilitator making retail sales to Washington consumers. D.C. directs facilitators to register for a marketplace sales tax account and begin collection immediately on marketplace sales. Confirm that account before offering taxable D.C. screenings. Record the authority, decision and effective date for each jurisdiction. [Marketplace facilitator state guidance](https://www.streamlinedsalestax.org/for-businesses/marketplace-facilitator) · [Washington Department of Revenue](https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/retail-sales-tax/marketplace-fairness-leveling-playing-field/marketplace-facilitators) · [D.C. Office of Tax and Revenue](https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/page/sales-and-use-tax-faqs)
4. **Register where required.** Use each tax agency’s marketplace or sales and use tax registration, or select applicable participating states through the Streamlined Sales Tax Registration System. Check local rules separately. For example, Alaska’s commission handles remote sales to participating municipalities once the statewide threshold is met. Save each permit, filing frequency, first due date and any seller notice requirement. [Streamlined registration](https://www.streamlinedsalestax.org/for-businesses/remote-seller-faqs) · [Alaska commission](https://arsstc.org/business-sellers/)
5. **Activate collection and assign filing.** Add each approved jurisdiction to the Dust Wave platform’s live Stripe Tax settings with the correct effective date. A test registration only exercises checkout. Assign someone to file returns and remit on the agency’s schedule, including zero returns where required. Include manually calculated New Mexico orders in the filing totals. Verify a rental, a purchase and the inclusive platform tip for each new collection configuration. [Stripe registration](https://docs.stripe.com/tax/registering) · [New Mexico order reporting](new-mexico-grt.md)

## Paying the tax authority

One low-cost option in New Mexico is to file the GRT return and pay through Taxpayer Access Point on Dust Wave’s assigned schedule. Reconcile both manual and automatic orders once, along with other reportable activity of the same entity. [New Mexico filing and payment](https://www.tax.newmexico.gov/businesses/how-to-make-a-payment/)

Stripe Tax Basic covers calculation. Stripe’s built-in U.S. filing service requires Tax Complete and separate TaxJar enrollment for each jurisdiction. Check the first filing period shown as active and cover any earlier returns. Once enrollment is approved, TaxJar files and remits from the authorized U.S. bank account on the filing schedule. New Mexico enrollment also calls for a signed Tax Information Authorization. Stripe excludes manual-tax-rate orders from automatic filing data by default. Import those transactions and verify their location and totals on the draft return. Check the new fee schedule if you change plans. [Stripe U.S. filing, remittance and imports](https://docs.stripe.com/tax/file-with-stripe) · [New Mexico TaxJar enrollment](https://support.taxjar.com/article/702-what-do-i-need-to-enroll-in-autofile-for-new-mexico) · [Stripe Tax plan pricing](https://stripe.com/tax/pricing)

## After launch

Track gross film sales and transaction counts by destination across filmmakers and relevant channels. Use alerts before each applicable threshold and review new physical locations or filmmaker states when they change. Stripe documents a monitoring gap for Stream’s separate charges and transfers without `on_behalf_of`. Reconcile the marketplace ledger and any other sales channels to the state totals independently. A location showing zero Stripe tax does not establish that a permit is unnecessary. [Stripe monitoring and Connect coverage](https://docs.stripe.com/tax/monitoring) · [State marketplace rules](https://www.streamlinedsalestax.org/for-businesses/marketplace-facilitator)
